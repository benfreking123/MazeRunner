## Shop Commands
For Selling All Items <br>
`/genesis SellShop` <br>
For Buying Items <br>
`/genesis BuyShop` <br>
For Selling ProRPGItems <br>
`/sell open` <br>

## Worth
### Sell Shop
Items sold through the Genesis Sell Shop are sold through the essentials command /sell <item_name>
There worth is defined under plugins\essentials\worth.yml

### GenItem Sell
Defined through the Divinity worth file at plugins\Divinity\worth.yml
