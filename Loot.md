## Interval Chest
Maze Interval Loot Chest [Phatloot]<br>
Makes Loot Pool<br>
`/phatloots:loot make daily_chest_inner_common`<br>
Links Chest to Loot Pool<br>
`/phatloots:loot link daily_chest_inner_common` (Works on as many chest as you want)<br>
GUI Editor - to edit loot, the amount of items, etc.<br>
`/phatloots:loot gui daily_chest_inner_common`<br>
