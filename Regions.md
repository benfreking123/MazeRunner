World Guard Regions
## Region Commands
Create Region:<br>
`/rg create <name>`<br>
Entry Title Command:<br>
`/rg flag -w "world" thefield console-command-on-entry "/title %username% title {"text":"The Field","color":"gold","bold":true}"`<br>
